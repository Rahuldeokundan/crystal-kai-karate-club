About the club

More than
martial arts.

Crystal Kai Karate Club is a member of Japan Shito-Ryu Kohshi Kai Karate Do India (JSKKI). Led by Sensei Sandhir Banerjei, the club focuses on physical fitness, discipline, confidence and practical self-defence.

Our classes help children build resilience, improve concentration and turn screen time into active, real-life skills in a safe and positive environment.

Belt Test Competition

Nearly 90 players advanced to their next belt.

Students demonstrated outstanding discipline, strength and technique at the Sector 40 Center Park belt test held by the Gautam Buddha Nagar unit of JSKKI.

Competition winners included: Anaya, Angel, Kavir, Ajit, Hanshika, Adhiraj, Naitik, Bhumika Pandey, Yash Prajapati and Chetan Gautam.
